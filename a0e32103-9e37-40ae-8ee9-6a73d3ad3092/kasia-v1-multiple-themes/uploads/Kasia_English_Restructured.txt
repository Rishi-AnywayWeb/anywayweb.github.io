About Me

For many years, I found myself in relationships that looked like love
to me, but in reality lacked emotional safety and stability.

Growing up in a dysfunctional environment, I didn't have a clear model
for healthy emotional connection. Without realising it, I repeated
familiar patterns and became drawn into relationships that slowly
drained my energy and eroded my self-worth.

I used to believe something was wrong with me. Now I see it clearly: I
was responding to an unhealthy dynamic — nothing was broken within me.

As a survivor of narcissistic and emotional abuse, I understand
first-hand how overwhelming and isolating recovery can feel. My own
healing journey showed me how rare it can be to find professionals who
truly understand this experience. That is exactly why I chose to train
and specialise in this field — so you wouldn't have to search as long
as I did.

Through deep inner work, education, and time, I came to realise that
true recovery requires more than knowledge. It is about feeling safe,
supported, and truly seen while you rebuild yourself from within. That
realisation changed everything for me.

Today, I am a trauma-informed narcissistic abuse recovery practitioner
and hypnotherapist. I support others in reclaiming their inner strength
and reconnecting with their authentic self, safely and with compassion.

Originally from Poland, I have lived and worked abroad for over 25
years and feel deeply European at heart. I work fluently in English,
Dutch, and Polish, which allows me to support clients across different
cultures and backgrounds.

Before dedicating myself to this work, I spent 16 years in
Compensation & Benefits, working globally with people from diverse
cultural backgrounds. It grounded me in structure and strategy, and
taught me how to adapt and communicate effectively across different
cultures and perspectives.

Alongside my career, I've always been drawn to self-development,
wellbeing, travel, and practices that connect body and mind, such as
Pilates and Latin dance.

These experiences shape the way I work today: grounded, intuitive, and
guided by real human connection and awareness.
