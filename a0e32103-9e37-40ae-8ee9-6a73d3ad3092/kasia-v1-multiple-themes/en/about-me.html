Why I Do This Work

I understand narcissistic abuse not only through professional training, but through lived experience. That's exactly why this work is so personal to me — and why I care so much about getting it right for you.

I know what it feels like to lose yourself in a relationship. To question your reality, doubt your own intuition, and quietly wonder why you can't just let go.

What once felt impossible even to imagine became my reality: I healed. I found my inner peace and learned how to protect my energy, trust myself again, and build healthy, loving relationships based on respect and emotional safety.

As I rebuilt my own life, I became passionate about understanding why narcissistic abuse affects us so deeply, and what it truly takes to recover. This led me to immerse myself in trauma recovery, transformational coaching, hypnotherapy, and holistic healing. Combining professional training with lived experience allows me to bring both real knowledge and genuine understanding to this work.

My greatest strength is creating a space where you feel truly seen, understood, and supported. With warmth, compassion, and a trauma-informed approach, I help you reconnect with your inner strength and move forward with confidence.

You do not have to walk this path alone. I will walk alongside you, step by step, for as long as it takes, until you feel grounded in yourself again.

About Me

For many years, I found myself in relationships that looked like love to me, but in reality lacked emotional safety and stability.

Growing up in a dysfunctional environment, I didn't have a clear model for healthy emotional connection. Without realising it, I repeated familiar patterns and became drawn into relationships that slowly drained my energy and eroded my self-worth.

I used to believe something was wrong with me. Now I see it clearly: I was responding to an unhealthy dynamic — nothing was broken within me.

As a survivor of narcissistic and emotional abuse, I understand first-hand how overwhelming and isolating recovery can feel. My own healing journey showed me how rare it can be to find professionals who truly understand this experience. That is exactly why I chose to train and specialise in this field — so you wouldn't have to search as long as I did.

Through deep inner work, education, and time, I came to realise that true recovery requires more than knowledge. It is about feeling safe, supported, and truly seen while you rebuild yourself from within. That realisation changed everything for me.

Today, I am a trauma-informed narcissistic abuse recovery practitioner and hypnotherapist. I support others in reclaiming their inner strength and reconnecting with their authentic self, safely and with compassion.

Originally from Poland, I have lived and worked abroad for over 25 years and feel deeply European at heart. I work fluently in English, Dutch, and Polish, which allows me to support clients across different cultures and backgrounds.

Before dedicating myself to this work, I spent 16 years in Compensation & Benefits, working globally with people from diverse cultural backgrounds. It grounded me in structure and strategy, and taught me how to adapt and communicate effectively across different cultures and perspectives.

Alongside my career, I've always been drawn to self-development, wellbeing, travel, and practices that connect body and mind, such as Pilates and Latin dance.

These experiences shape the way I work today: grounded, intuitive, and guided by real human connection and awareness.

My Mission

My mission is to help survivors of narcissistic abuse reclaim their lives, reconnect with themselves, and heal with greater clarity, support, and confidence than I had in my own journey.

I believe recovery does not have to be a long and lonely process. With the right guidance, understanding, and tools, healing becomes not only possible, but truly transformative.

My work is dedicated to helping you:

  • Break free from self-doubt, confusion, and trauma bonds
  • Rebuild self-trust, healthy boundaries, and emotional resilience
  • Heal the wounds left by emotional and narcissistic abuse
  • Reconnect with your inner strength, intuition, and authentic self
  • Create a life rooted in peace, confidence, and healthy relationships

Everything I do is grounded in safety, compassion, honesty, and empowerment.

Because my mission is about more than helping you recover from what happened.

It is about helping you return to the version of yourself that was never truly lost — only buried beneath years of survival — so you can create the life you truly deserve.
