Recognize The Signs

Narcissistic abuse is often invisible — even to the person living it.

Below are some of the patterns people most often recognize once they finally have the words for what happened to them.

  • You often replay conversations, wondering what you did wrong
  • You've been called “too sensitive” or “too much”
  • You find it hard to trust your own memory or judgement
  • You feel drained or unlike yourself around one particular person

If any of this sounds familiar, you don't have to make sense of it alone.
