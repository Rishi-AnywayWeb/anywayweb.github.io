The Path to Healing

Healing from narcissistic abuse is not about forgetting what happened. It is about gently reclaiming the parts of yourself that got lost while you were trying to survive it.

You do not have to figure everything out on your own. Think of me as a guide for the part of the journey that feels impossible to walk alone.

Together, we work on the deeper patterns that keep you stuck — rather than staying in the story of what happened to you.

I help you to:

  • Regulate your nervous system and restore a sense of emotional safety
  • Break free from trauma bonds and emotional dependency
  • Identify unconscious patterns that attract unhealthy relationships
  • Rebuild self-worth, confidence, and self-trust
  • Reconnect with your inner wisdom and authentic self
  • Develop strong boundaries without guilt or fear
  • Create healthy, fulfilling relationships rooted in respect and authenticity

My work combines trauma-informed coaching and hypnotherapy, because healing something this deep asks for more than just talking about it.

Healing doesn't come from talking alone. Healing comes from the inner work.

Recovery is a process — and you don't have to navigate it alone.

  • Nervous system regulation and emotional healing tools
